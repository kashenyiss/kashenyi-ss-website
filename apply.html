Admissions

Your New Beginning Awaits

Kashenyi Secondary School welcomes students of all backgrounds who are eager to learn. Entry is open at:

  • Senior One (S.1)— for candidates who have completed Primary Seven (PLE).
  • Senior Five (S.5)— for students who have completed UCE, choosing a science or arts combination.
  • Transfers— students from other schools are considered at all levels, space permitting.

Admission is based on merit, conduct and available space. Complete the form below and our admissions office will guide you through the next steps.

1

Submit Application

Fill in the online form below or collect a paper form from the school office.

2

Attach Documents

Submit PLE/UCE results slip and birth certificate copies with the form.

3

Interview & Assessment

Shortlisted candidates take a simple assessment and brief interview.

4

Admission Letter

Successful applicants receive an admission letter and joining instructions.
