KSS Shines in UCE 2025 Results

Our candidates recorded a 94% pass rate, with a significant number qualifying for A-Level admission to top schools and our own A-Level stream. Several learners scored in Division One, and the school's mean aggregate improved for the fourth consecutive year — a testament to the dedication of our teachers and the hard work of our students.

U20 Football Team Shows Great Spirit

Our U20 football team continues to make the school proud, training hard and representing Kashenyi in inter-school competitions across the district. The team embodies our motto — Struggle for Progress — on and off the pitch.

Construction of New Girls' Dormitory Underway

Work has officially begun on a new, modern girls' dormitory that will expand our boarding capacity and provide better living and study spaces for our learners. The project is a proud milestone for the school and the entire community, made possible through the support of parents, the Board of Governors and well-wishers. We look forward to handing over the facility to our students.

Alumni Fund Sponsors 20 New Bursaries

The Kashenyi Alumni Association has launched a bursary fund that will support 20 bright but needy students through O-Level. Applications are being received by the school office — a proud moment for the Kashenyi family that gives back.
